I'm Mel, a cybersecurity researcher and technologist working across security research, threat modeling, and emerging technologies.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how complex systems behave, where their assumptions break down, and what we can learn by examining problems beyond their traditional boundaries.

<!-- FIXME: Replace with a direct link to the TDFF project page once available. -->
My background includes vulnerability research, automated security testing, and network architecture. My work ranges from [taint-driven firmware fuzzing](/projects/) to exploring how we document and understand connected environments, including my [IETF Internet-Draft on residential network mapping](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-savich-residential-network-map/).

Beyond the technical, my curiosity extends to the intersections of technology, human behavior, and the systems that shape our world. I'm especially interested in questions surrounding identity, authenticity, and trust, and what fields like sociology, psychology, and philosophy can contribute to how we approach security.
