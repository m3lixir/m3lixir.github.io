<i>Curious by nature. Skeptical by default.</i>

I'm Mel, a cybersecurity strategist and technologist focused on understanding how systems work, where they fail, and what happens when they're pushed beyond their intended boundaries.

My professional interests span threat modeling, secure architecture, privacy, threat intelligence, and emerging technologies. I'm particularly drawn to unconventional attack surfaces, overlooked vulnerabilities, and the intersection of technology, human behavior, and security.

I approach cybersecurity with the belief that effective security requires more than implementing controls or following established frameworks. It demands understanding how systems behave in practice, questioning assumptions, and anticipating threats that aren't immediately apparent.

Beyond cybersecurity, I explore OSINT, artificial intelligence, automation, network analysis, and independent development. I enjoy experimenting with unfamiliar technologies, challenging conventional approaches, and finding creative solutions to complex problems.

This website is a collection of my professional work, technical projects, research, writing, and ongoing experiments. It's where I document what I'm building, share what I'm learning, and explore ideas worth investigating.

<b>Good questions. Strange places.</b>
