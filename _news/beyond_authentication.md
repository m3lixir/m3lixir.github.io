I'm introducing **Beyond Authentication**, a series exploring identity, trust, and the systems we haven't built yet. The starting question: _what does proving who we're interacting with actually tell us about whether to trust the interaction?_ I'll follow that question through security, human behavior, and the possibilities—and limits—of designing systems that help us decide.
